## Distributor Review After Deep Testing (2026-10-15)
This is an operational risk review, not a claim of professional distribution experience. Browser evidence and test scope are in `Progress.md`; reproducible defects and fixes are in `TODO.md`. Recommendations below are not approved feature designs.

### Before Real Stock Is Trusted
- **Goods must never appear from status changes.** Delivering an 18-case order from a count of 14, then reopening it, created 4 cases in the browser. Fixed under the user's ruling: dispatch is blocked until the count is corrected (`setOrderStatus`, `WorkspaceScreen`). Still design structured dispatch/receipt movements before allowing partial delivery, returns or edits to closed orders.
- **Receiving capacity must survive receiving: fixed.** `receiveDelivery` automatically records actual quantities and confirmation times in `Workspace.goodsReceipts`, with no upload. `rehearse` combines received transfers and incoming bookings by local day across products, including partial receipts. Browser confirmation plus reload kept 8 of 8 slots used. This is not a full dispatch/returns ledger; old arrivals are not inferred from History and incoming transfer identity still relies on a supplier-name prefix (`GoodsReceipt`, `addHistory`, `InboundShipment`).
- **Distinguish on-hand from expected: fixed.** A 10-case order with zero on hand and 10 arriving on time now says Watch with its dependency note, even without a delay. `orderCoverage.onHandCovered` and `rehearse` separate stock-only coverage from total coverage; browser Orders and Overview rechecked. A shortage on another product keeps the order at risk (`CommitmentsPanel`, `IncidentOverview`).
- **Misleading deadlines: fixed; stale offers still open.** `rehearse` derives Book by from the earliest order the transfer actually helps; an already-covered priority order no longer rejects the transfer or sets its deadline. A still-short priority order's timing rule is unchanged and the late-but-useful exception still needs its separate ruling. Supplier offers still use fixed arrival timestamps without quote expiry (`SupplierOffer`, existing TODO ruling).

### Operational Capabilities To Design
- **Sellable stock, not just counted stock:** batches, expiry/FEFO, damaged/quarantined goods, safety stock and substitution. `StockRecord` currently has one count per product/location and `Product` has no expiry or substitute relationship. This matters for food/pharmacy stock even when arithmetic is correct.
- **A realistic replenishment promise:** supplier minimum quantities, carton/pallet multiples, order cutoffs, working days, loading time and truck capacity. `SupplierOffer` has quantity/price/arrival; `TransferRoute` has elapsed hours and costs, not these constraints. A feasible 6-package rescue may not be purchasable or loadable as proposed.
- **Partial dispatch and customer backorders:** `Order` supports open/delivered/cancelled, not quantities dispatched, remaining or returned. Decide whether to send 6 now and 4 later, promise a revised date, substitute stock or split warehouses. Do not overload Reopen to mean a physical return.
- **Capacity units:** one `receivingLimitPerDay` is shared across unlike products. Adding cartons of televisions and bags of rice is a package count, not a measure of unloading effort or weight. Choose dock slots, pallets, weight or a clearly declared package convention before treating this as a fleet/dock model (`Location`, `rehearse`).
- **Use projected storage at arrival:** rescue caps use today's on-hand count, not scheduled inbound or planned consumption. Receiving a shipment has no storage-capacity gate. Define over-capacity warnings separately from whether staff may record the true receipt (`storageCap`, `rehearse`, `receiveDelivery`).
- **Commercial decisions:** the worth warning compares rescue cost with selling revenue, not margin; no order-level margin, penalty or credit model exists. Keep the warning advisory, and do not describe Lowest cost as maximum profitability (`Product.pricePerUnit`, `Goal`, `rankPlans`).
- **Long-running operations:** History drops entries after 500; Orders/Suppliers/History lack Inventory's search/paging. Plan reconciliation and durable audit requirements before presenting this as the business's primary stock system (`LIMITS.history`, `addHistory`; scale observation in `Progress.md`).

### Verification Boundaries
The 16-case browser matrix includes views as well as workflows; it is not exhaustive. The seeded week does not explore all action sequences: it missed dispatching an order with insufficient stock, which browser testing caught. Real Google login, guest-to-account transfer on a real account, two-device concurrency, live Gemini extraction accuracy and production deployment were not validated in this pass. Existing automated storage/rules tests are useful guards, not substitutes for those live flows.

## 2026-10-14
- **Add location confirmation:** For a new location, show that it will be created and list its details. Avoid `Unknown -> ...` and existing-value changes, which make the flow look like an edit. Clarify the impact, for example: “This adds a location; existing plans won’t change.”
- **Add product fields:** Clarify that “Pack” describes the package (for example, “20 bars per box”) and “Counted in” is the unit used for stock quantities (“box” or “bar”). A number like `20` alone is ambiguous.
- **Product form wording:** Rename “Pack” and “Counted in” to clearer labels, and show helper text with an example beneath each field. Feedback only for now; do not change the app yet.
- **Inventory UI feedback:** The product dropdown and location cards make the relationship hard to scan. Avoid a full product-by-location grid and a tree; both make it harder to navigate at scale.
- **Inventory scale constraint:** The inventory view must remain usable with around 100 products and 100 locations; a full product-by-location matrix would expose 10,000 cells and be difficult to scan.
- **Agreed inventory layout:** Add a two-way toggle, **By product** / **By warehouse**. In product mode, search products and see stock across warehouses; in warehouse mode, search warehouses and see products at that location. Keep each view searchable and paginated so the UI never shows all 10,000 possible records at once. Approved 2026-10-14.
- **Add order review:** The confirmation step omits Product and Ships from (they keep their preselected defaults, so they aren't shown as changes). For a new order, show every field so the user can verify what they're saving. Same on Add incoming shipment: Product and Arriving at are missing from the review.
- **Order priority wording:** The field is "Priority" and one option is also "Priority" (the other is "Standard"), which reads as "Priority: Priority". Consider options like "High priority" / "Standard".
- **Receiving limit is unclear:** Users read it as storage capacity ("Wakanda already has stock, will it overflow?"). It is actually an unloading limit per day, and the planner only uses it to cap emergency transfers (shared/planner/plan.ts). It does not check supplier shipments, other arrivals on the same day, or existing stock. It is also one number shared by every product, shown as "200 bags/day" for Vibranium and "200 units/day" for Gun, and never added up across products (200 bags + 200 units on the same day both pass). Clarify the label (e.g. "Most it can unload per day") and decide whether storage capacity is needed.
- **Proposal (not decided): per-item limit per warehouse.** Example: "Wakanda can hold at most 500 bags of Vibranium". Suggested placement is an optional field on the stock record (product x warehouse), not the Add product form, which doesn't know the warehouses and would need 100 fields for 100 warehouses. Leaving it blank means no limit. Alternative: a product-level default with per-warehouse overrides. Open question: is this a storage limit (how much fits) or a receiving limit (how much can be unloaded per day)?
  - User direction (2026-10-14): storage matters, likely both. Storage = new, optional, per product x warehouse; warn when stock + incoming exceeds it. Unloading = keep the existing per-warehouse limit and fix its label. Build storage first if only one.
- **Supplier arrival should be a lead time, not a fixed date:** The backup supplier's "Arrival" is a fixed date entered at setup. The planner already uses it correctly (rejects the supplier as "too late" if it can't arrive before the affected orders are due; asks for it if blank, shared/planner/plan.ts). But a backup supplier's delivery time really depends on when you place the order, so a fixed date goes stale. Proposal: ask "Can deliver within ___ days/hours" and compute arrival from the time of the delay.
  - User point (2026-10-14): at setup we don't know if we'll ever order from the backup supplier, so its stock, price and arrival are guesses that go stale. The real question ("can you deliver 45 bags to Wakanda by 20 Oct 10 AM?") only exists once a delay happens. Proposed flow (not decided): setup records only who / what / where, plus an optional typical lead time as a rough guide. When a delay happens, the supplier option shows "Ask supplier" with a drafted message containing the exact quantity and deadline. The option stays "waiting for supplier" until the user records the reply (typed in, or attached and read by Gemini). Only a confirmed reply makes it ready.

For limits, blank means unlimited, so "Unknown" is wrong. Use "No limit (∞)" on Most packages it can store here and Most it can unload per day. Orders are only compared with stock inside a reported delay (CommitmentsPanel.tsx, shared/planner/plan.ts).   - Progress: the check exists in the planner (orderCoverage in shared/planner/plan.ts, tested) but is not shown on screen yet. Warning must say "no incoming shipment recorded", not "nothing is coming": the app only knows shipments the user entered.
- **Repeating deliveries (proposal, not decided):** today every incoming shipment is a one-time entry, so a warehouse restocked every Monday needs a new entry each week.
