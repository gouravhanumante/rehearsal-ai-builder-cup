# 01 — Hackathon Facts

Source: https://aibuildercup.com/ (overview, themes, rewards and FAQ pages) and organiser
emails, read on 2026-10-13. Re-check the site before submitting; rules can change.

## Event
- Google Cloud AI Builder Cup 2026, run by Hack2skill, sponsored by Google Cloud.
- Region: JAPAC. Working professionals only — **students are not eligible** (disqualification,
  even after shortlisting).
- Team of 2–4. Only 2 members travel to the finale.
- Only **fresh work** built during the hackathon window counts. No pre-existing projects.
- One team submits **one** solution under **one** theme.
- All materials in English.

## Themes (pick exactly one)
1. BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences
2. **Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences** — mentions demand
   planning, inventory, operational efficiency across physical and digital commerce.
3. **Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency** — mentions efficiency,
   reliability, supply and demand; turning operational data into insights, recommendations
   or actions.
4. Media, Content & Digital Experiences
5. Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity
6. Sustainability & Social Impact

Rehearsal fits **2** or **3**. Which one is **OPEN** — see 05-scope-and-decisions.md.

## Technical requirements
- Must use Google AI models (Gemini or Gemma) or Google agentic platforms (Agent Platform,
  Antigravity, AI Studio).
- Must be deployed on Google Cloud via **Cloud Run** or **Firebase**.
- The written description must explain how we use **Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Run and
  Gemini** — so all four must genuinely be in the build.

## Mandatory submission (all five, or the entry is incomplete)
1. **Pitch deck** on the prescribed template (file: "Submission Template _ AI Builder
   Cup.pptx", provided by organisers). Not following the template can disqualify.
2. **Demo video** showing the working solution (length: see conflict below).
3. **Live deployed prototype link** — must keep working throughout evaluation. No retries if
   anything (app, API, integration) is down.
4. **Public GitHub repository** with source and supporting material.
5. **Brief description** of what we built, including how Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Run and
   Gemini are used.

If the video does not clearly show working functionality, the entry does not progress to
prototype evaluation.

## Judging criteria
| Criterion | Weight | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Technical merit & Gen AI implementation | 40% | Meaningful Gen AI use, technically sound, scalable |
| Problem alignment & impact | 25% | Fits the theme, real value to users |
| Innovation & creativity | 25% | Fresh approach, non-superficial Gen AI use |
| User experience & solution design | 10% | Intuitive, accessible, components work together |

## Prizes
Winner USD 10,000 · 1st runner-up 7,000 · 2nd runner-up 5,000 · Best use of Google Cloud AI
tools 2,000 · Most Impactful 2,000 · Social Choice 2,000 · Best UI/UX 2,000.
Finalists get sponsored travel and stay in Singapore.

## Known conflicts in the organisers' material (confirm on Discord before submitting)
- **Video length:** email says 3–4 minutes; FAQ says "under 3 minutes"; themes page says
  "3 minutes". Safest target: **about 3:00**, never over 4:00.
- **Category label:** themes page says to name the category as "Healthcare, Education,
  Sustainability, Accessibility, Social Good" — this does not match the six themes and looks
  like leftover text. Use the theme name.
- Themes page asks for the deck "converted into a PDF"; the email asks for the template.
  Likely both: fill the template, export to PDF. Confirm.
